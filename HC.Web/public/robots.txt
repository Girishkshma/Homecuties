# robots.txt for the Homecuties storefront (https://homecuties.com)
#
# Everything a shopper may open is open to a crawler: the home page, the shop and
# every collection in it, a product page and the two policy pages. There is
# deliberately no Disallow list here: the pages that are never worth listing - the
# cart, the checkout, the account pages and the password forms - are served with
# 'noindex, follow' in their heads instead (see src/app/app-routing.module.ts). A
# page blocked here is never fetched, so its noindex is never read, and its
# address can still be listed with nothing under it.
#
# The sitemap lists the pages that exist before anything is loaded from the API.
# Product and collection pages are discovered by following the shop's own links.
