Categories

The catalogue of the shop. A heading names a collection ('Decoratives') and the shelves beneath it ('Vases', 'Pot Houses') are what products are filed under: nothing is filed on a heading itself, because a customer who opens one browses everything filed beneath it - which is what the storefront's menus are built from.

Renaming a category changes it wherever it is read (the menus, the product form, a product's own 'Decoratives / Vases' label). Moving one carries its shelves with it. Taking one out is refused while anything still sits under it or is filed on it.
