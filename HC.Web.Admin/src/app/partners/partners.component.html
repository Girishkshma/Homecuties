Partners

Partners are business entities (sellers/merchants) who list and sell their products on the Homecuties platform. Each partner can have multiple users assigned with different roles (e.g., Admin, Manager, Inventory Clerk) who manage their inventory, orders, and product listings. Partners are the primary sellers on the platform and are responsible for fulfilling orders placed by customers.
