Portal Access

Where the shop is being read from, and how often. Every page view the storefront opens is recorded with the address it came from and the place that address is in - a location and a count, never a person: no customer is named, shown or exported here.

Both days empty is the month to date. A period wider than a year is read a year at a time, and a period whose days run backwards is read as the single day it names. Everything is read in UTC, which is how the visits are recorded - the log says so too rather than quietly shifting the hours.

Keeping the history

Every page view is kept until it is cleared out here - there is no customer data in these rows to keep or to delete, only an address, a place and a time. Clearing the old ones keeps the table (and the report) quick; the retention is never shorter than a month, because the report reads the month to date.

days

The server removes in batches and never less than 30 days or more than 10 years at a time, so a slip of a digit cannot empty the table and the storefront never waits on the delete.
